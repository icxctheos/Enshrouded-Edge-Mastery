## ✨ Core Capabilities

Our framework is divided into four distinct pillars, each addressing a critical aspect of the Enshrouded experience.

### 🌫️ 1. Shroud-Sight Analytics
- **Live Hazard Mapping**: Visualizes the density and direction of Shroud corruption in your immediate vicinity, allowing for proactive route planning.
- **Depth Perception Aid**: Calculates the vertical drop of sinkholes and ancient ruins with a **precision measurement tool** that mimics a surveyor’s laser, preventing fatal falls.
- **Spore Bloom Forecasting**: Predicts the 60-second window before major fungal eruptions, granting you the foresight to reposition or engage.

### ⚔️ 2. Combat Rhythm Modulator
- **Parry Window Visualizer**: Overlays a subtle, customizable timing marker on enemy wind-up animations, helping you land perfect blocks with **muscle-memory reinforcement**.
- **Stamina Efficiency Index**: Displays a real-time percentage of stamina consumption per action, allowing you to optimize your attack chains and dodges for prolonged engagements.
- **Aggro Radius Delineator**: Draws invisible boundaries around hostile camps, revealing the precise edge of their territorial instinct, so you can lure or bypass with expert control.

### 🏰 3. Construction & Resource Alchemist
- **Structural Integrity Gauge**: Instantly evaluates the load-bearing capacity of your current build, identifying weak points before they become catastrophic collapses.
- **Harvesting Yield Calculator**: Cross-references your tools and perks to project the most efficient extraction path for rare ores and fibers, minimizing wasted swings.
- **Flame Altar Efficiency Monitor**: Tracks the innate power output of your altar across different times of day, suggesting optimal placement for maximum buff potency.

### 🌐 4. Companion Intelligence Interface
- **Multi-Language Nexus**: Supports 12 major languages, from English and German to Japanese and Portuguese, ensuring the entire community can navigate the tool with ease. The interface automatically adapts to your system’s locale, but manual overrides are always available.
- **Alert Priority Queue**: Customizable notification system that filters environmental changes, from chest spawns to elite enemy movements, into a non-intrusive ticker.

## 🛠️ Framework Architecture

This repository is structured for modularity and longevity. Each feature operates as an independent module, communicating through a central event bus.

```
/enshrouded-guardian
├── /core
│   ├── /hazard-mapper       # Shroud-sight engine
│   ├── /combat-rhythm       # Parry & stamina logic
│   ├── /construction-aid    # Build integrity solver
│   └── /companion-ui        # Interface rendering layer
├── /data
│   ├── /lang               # Translation files (.json)
│   └── /profiles           # User-specific calibration presets
├── /docs
│   └── /walkthrough        # In-depth visual guides for each module
└── CHANGELOG.md            # Versioned release notes with semantic progress
```

The **event bus** architecture ensures that disabling one module (e.g., Combat Rhythm) does not impact the stability of the others. This decoupling is intentional—it allows you to build your own **guardian loadout** over time.
